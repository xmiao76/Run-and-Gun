# Asset and Intellectual-Property Policy

## 1. Non-negotiable rule

This repository must not contain or reproduce protected Contra expression. Do not use:

- Contra, Probotector, Konami, or related titles as the public game name;
- logos, character designs, enemy designs, boss designs, story text, UI text, sprites, animations, tiles, backgrounds, maps, encounter sequences, music, sound effects, fonts, screenshots, box art, or ROM data copied or traced from the games;
- assets extracted from ROMs, emulators, videos, fan sites, wikis, sprite sheets, or gameplay captures;
- close pixel-for-pixel recreations made by observing the original.

Broad genre mechanics such as running, jumping, eight-direction shooting, weapon pickups, checkpoints, enemies, and side-scrolling levels may inspire the design, but all concrete expression must be original.

This is the line the project works to: **mechanics and conventions may be emulated; specific expression may not.** A deliberate effort to make the game *feel* like a classic run-and-gun is in scope and always has been — `PROJECT.md` calls for exactly that. Reproducing any particular game's art, audio, layouts, or names is not, however closely the result is described as "inspired by".

## 2. Allowed sources

An asset may be committed only when it is one of:

- created specifically for this project;
- generated procedurally by project code;
- generated by an authorized tool and permitted for this use;
- public domain;
- licensed under terms that permit redistribution and modification in this repository.

## 4. Audio

All audio is synthesized at runtime; no audio files are shipped. Any future audio must have documented provenance. Do not imitate or transcribe recognizable melodies from Contra or any other commercial work — including rhythm, interval sequence, and instrumentation that would be recognized as a quotation.

## 5. Naming

Working/public title: **Operation Iron Echo**.

Use original names for the player, enemies, weapons, bosses, levels, and organizations. Avoid names that imply official association or a licensed remake.
